# Field test guide

For testing the app in your own markets. Nothing here needs a computer once the site is published.

## 1. Put it on the phone
1. Open the site address in **Safari** (not an in-app browser).
2. Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Open it from the new icon from now on: Safari keeps data for installed apps more reliably than for tabs.
3. Settings → **Allow lookups**. This turns on map pictures, building search and address search (OpenStreetMap). Nothing is sent until you do.

## 2. Set it up once (about five minutes)
- **Settings → My details**: name, company, mailing address, phone, reply-to email. Emails and quotes use these.
- **Settings → Services & your prices**: type your own rate for each service you quote (window cleaning exterior floor 1, floors 2 and up, and so on). No price is built in, so a line without a rate stays blank.
- **Floor height**: only needed if you price per square foot of facade.
- **Settings → Demonstration data → Clear demo data**, so only real leads remain.
- Settings → **Export backup** now and then. Everything lives on the phone.

## 3. A first run through a market
1. **Territory Map**: go to an address or tap to drop a pin. Pick a radius (start with 1 mile), a quick pick (for example *Offices* or *Hotels*) and Search.
2. Read the ranked list. Tap a result to see it in **Property Intelligence**. **Add** the ones worth working.
3. In each property: confirm floors, building edge and size on site, add owner, manager and decision maker as you learn them (each fact says whether it is a guess or verified), take a photo, add a note.
4. **Draft quote** → fill any blank line (your rate, floors, building edge) → save → read → approve. **Prepare outreach** → it opens the draft in your mail app → send it yourself → tap **I sent it**.
5. Move the lead through the pipeline as it progresses. Check **Dashboard** for follow-ups due and today's route.

## 4. What to watch and tell us
- **Search quality**: does the list match what you know of the area? Are good targets missing? Is the order sensible? (Many commercial buildings have no floor count on the map: use *Include buildings with no floor count*.)
- **Numbers**: floors, size and building edge are from volunteer-mapped data. Note where they were wrong on site.
- **Search errors**: a very large radius can time out. Try a smaller one. Note the place and radius if it keeps failing.
- **The quote**: does the way it adds up match how you really price? What is missing?
- **Anything slow, confusing, or too small to tap** on your phone.
- **Does your data stay put** after you close and reopen the app?

Write notes in the app (a lead's Notes tab) or send them to the builders; please don't paste real customer details into public places. The repository is public.

## 5. Seamless.ai and Trellis (planned)
- **Seamless.ai** (emails and phone numbers for decision makers): a later version imports the CSV you export, into existing property records, tagged "Seamless.ai" with the date and never marked verified until you confirm. The file stays on your phone.
- **Trellis** (call transcripts): a later version attaches a transcript to the property and offers a short "what I heard" list (decision maker, timing, current vendor, objections, next step) that you accept or reject. Nothing is added unreviewed.
- A live connection to either would be a new outside request and needs your approval first. Check recording-consent rules for the states you call into before recording.
